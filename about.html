Since 2016

Technology & Training, Rooted in Africa

We started with a simple belief: African enterprises deserve world-class technology infrastructure and professional development — delivered by people who understand the local context.

Our Mission

To bridge the technology and skills gap across Africa by delivering enterprise-grade infrastructure, accredited professional training, and strategic consultancy that drives measurable business outcomes.

We do not sell boxes. We architect solutions. We do not run courses. We build capabilities.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted technology and training partner for African enterprises, governments, and educational institutions — known for technical excellence, integrity, and outcomes that matter.

By 2030, we aim to certify 10,000 African professionals and deploy technology infrastructure in 20 markets.

Our Journey

2016

Ivy Africa Founded

Registered in Aliwal North, Eastern Cape. Started as a small IT training provider with 3 instructors and a single classroom.

2017

First Enterprise Contract

Delivered CompTIA A+ and Network+ training to a provincial government department — 60 delegates over 3 months.

2018

PECB Partnership

Became an authorised PECB training partner, adding ISO 27001, ISO 22301, and GDPR programmes to our catalog.

2019

Technology Division Launch

Added enterprise hardware sales — Dell, Lenovo, and HP — with a focus on public sector and education tenders.

2021

CompTIA Authorised Partner

Achieved CompTIA Authorised Partner status. Expanded course catalog to 14 CompTIA certifications.

2022

Huawei & Eaton Partnerships

Added networking (Huawei) and power protection (Eaton) to our technology portfolio. First data centre build project.

2023

K12 EdTech & Robotics

Launched coding & robotics programmes for schools. Built 3 STEM labs in the Eastern Cape. Added SACE-aligned teacher training.

2024

ISACA & Snapplify

Became ISACA course reseller. Added CISA, CISM, CRISC. Partnered with Snapplify for SACE educator CPD programmes.

2025

Pan-African Expansion

Active operations in 8 markets. 2,400+ professionals trained. 180+ enterprise clients. New consultancy division launched.

2026

Today

49 certification courses. Full-stack technology partner. ISO consultancy practice. SMME enablement programmes. And we are just getting started.
