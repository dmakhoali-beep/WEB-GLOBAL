Frequently Asked Questions

Do exam vouchers come with the course? +

Exam vouchers are quoted separately and can be bundled with your course booking. We provide guidance on scheduling your exam at a Pearson VUE or PECB/ISACA testing centre.

What is the pass rate for your delegates? +

Our first-time pass rate averages 94% across all vendors. We attribute this to hands-on labs, scenario-based practice exams, and post-course support via our alumni Slack channel.

Can you train at our office? +

Yes. Our on-site corporate delivery requires a minimum of 6 delegates. We bring all equipment, materials, and lab environments. Travel costs are quoted based on location.

Do you offer payment plans? +

Corporate clients can request 30-day payment terms. Individual learners can split payment 50/50 — 50% deposit to secure seat, 50% before course start date.

Are SACE CPD points guaranteed? +

All Snapplify educator courses are SACE-aligned and carry verified CPD points. Certificates are issued within 5 business days of course completion and can be uploaded directly to the SACE portal.
